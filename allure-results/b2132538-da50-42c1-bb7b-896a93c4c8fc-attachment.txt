AI Response:  Playwright is a Node.js library to automate Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit browsers with a single API. It allows testing web applications for functionality, rendering, and performance across various environments from a single test codebase. With Playwright, you can write end-to-end tests, automate browser interactions, and gather metrics on the performance of your web application. It's widely used in the industry for both testing and scraping purposes due to its flexibility, reliability, and ease of use.
